Common questions about AerisGo

How can I book a flight?

Select your departure city, arrival city, date and passengers, then choose an available flight.

Can I select my seat?

Yes. Seat selection is available after choosing a flight.

When does check-in open?

Your ticket page shows when check-in is available for your flight.
